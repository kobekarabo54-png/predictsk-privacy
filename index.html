PredictSK Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

In short: PredictSK does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, phone number or location. The app shows football match information and statistical predictions. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device and advertising data as described below.

Who we are

PredictSK ("the app", "we") is developed by Karabo Kobe, an independent developer based in Botswana.

Contact: kobekarabo54@gmail.com

What the app does

PredictSK shows upcoming football fixtures and recent results, and statistical estimates for those matches. The estimates are calculated on your phone from public match data. They are for information and entertainment only. They are not betting advice and are not guaranteed to be correct.

PredictSK is not a gambling app. You cannot place bets, deposit money or win money in it. The app is intended for people aged 18 and over.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • No name, email address, phone number or home address.
  • No precise or approximate location.
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data.
  • No payment information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your phone only. It is not sent to us:

  • Match data downloaded for display and calculation.
  • Your settings, such as the 12-hour or 24-hour time format.
  • The time your SK Picks access, earned by watching an ad, started and ends.

You can remove this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings or by uninstalling the app.

Third-party services

ServiceWhy we use itWhat it may receive
Google AdMob Shows banner ads and rewarded ads that fund the app. Your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, app usage related to ads, and approximate region derived from the IP address. AdMob may use this to show and measure ads.
Public sports data provider Supplies fixtures, scores and results. Your phone's IP address, as with any internet request. No personal data from the app is sent.

Google handles data for advertising under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can see how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Advertising choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings, under Settings, then Google, then Ads. If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

Rewarded ads

To open SK Picks, you watch one rewarded ad. Access lasts three hours. If an ad cannot be loaded several times in a row, access opens for 30 minutes so you are not blocked.

Children

PredictSK is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help.

Data security and retention

Because the app keeps its data on your device, we hold no personal data about you on our own servers. Data collected by Google AdMob is kept and protected by Google under its own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what data is held about you, to have it deleted, or to object to its use for advertising. We do not hold personal data about you ourselves, but you can contact us with any request and we will point you to the right place, including Google's own tools at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we will say so in the app.
